# Story-Ideen – Bildprompts für ChatGPT

Ein Bild pro Idee, jeweils als Triptychon entlang der Track-Dramaturgie
(Intro/Groove → Breakdown → Drop). Zuerst den Stilblock als eigene Nachricht senden,
dann die Ideen einzeln („Bild 1: …“). Englisch, weil die Bildmodelle darauf stabiler reagieren.

## Stilblock (erste Nachricht)

```
I'll send you 10 music-video concepts, one per message. For each, generate ONE image, 16:9,
laid out as a horizontal triptych of three equal panels separated by thin paper-white gutters:
LEFT = "groove" (calm, building), MIDDLE = "breakdown" (sparse, tense, empty), RIGHT = "drop"
(maximum energy, explosive).

Style for ALL images, keep it consistent:
- Risograph print look: visible coarse halftone dots, 3–5 flat spot-color plates, slight
  misregistration between plates (colors offset by a few pixels), overprint where plates overlap,
  grainy paper texture, ink density variations.
- Palette ONLY: fluorescent pink, fluorescent orange, aqua, sunflower yellow, on dark
  overprinted backgrounds made of midnight blue + purple + indigo. No pure black anywhere.
- On top: VHS rip artefacts — RGB chroma split, faint scanlines, one horizontal tracking band,
  small glitch tears, tape noise. Stronger in the right panel.
- Synthwave / glitchcore mood, flat graphic shapes, simple geometry, no photorealism,
  no text or lettering unless I ask for it.
Confirm briefly, then wait for concept 1.
```

## Die 10 Ideen

1. **The Copier.** `Concept 1 – "The Copier": a risograph machine printing this very video. LEFT: a single aqua plate, one sheet of paper sliding out of the machine, sparse halftone. MIDDLE: paper jam — crumpled sheet, plates badly out of register, everything drifting apart. RIGHT: all four fluo plates snapping into perfect register, a blast of sheets flying out of the machine toward the viewer, toner dust exploding.`
2. **Lighthouse.** `Concept 2 – "Lighthouse in a storm": night coastline, lighthouse on a cliff. LEFT: the beam sweeping slowly, rolling swell, spray as halftone dots. MIDDLE: calm dark sea, beam off, a single lightning bolt knocked out of all dark plates. RIGHT: the beam pointing straight into the camera, the whole frame flooded sunflower yellow, huge wave crashing.`
3. **Elevator.** `Concept 3 – "Elevator through a skyscraper": view from inside an elevator cabin. LEFT: doors slightly open, floor counter, streaks of light passing through the gap. MIDDLE: power outage, only pink emergency light, cabin almost empty. RIGHT: doors burst open onto a rooftop with a synthwave skyline and a huge striped sun.`
4. **Facade.** `Concept 4 – "Tower facade at night": frontal view of a high-rise, grid of windows. LEFT: lower floors lit in orange and aqua like an equalizer. MIDDLE: all dark except one single window high up with a dancing silhouette. RIGHT: the entire facade strobing, every window blazing in fluo colors, light rays spilling out.`
5. **Deep sea.** `Concept 5 – "Deep sea descent": a tiny submarine sinking. LEFT: aqua shallow water, bubbles, plankton, concentric sonar rings. MIDDLE: midnight/indigo void, only the sub's small light. RIGHT: a gigantic bioluminescent jellyfish creature in fluo pink erupting in front of the sub, pressure-wave rings everywhere.`
6. **Arcade.** `Concept 6 – "Arcade machine": a curved CRT screen of a space shooter, pixel sprites. LEFT: player ship shooting, rows of pixel invaders, scrolling starfield. MIDDLE: blinking "GAME OVER" screen with "INSERT COIN" (text allowed here). RIGHT: a massive pixel boss filling the screen, explosions, laser beams, heavy glitch.`
7. **Weather report.** `Concept 7 – "Weather report 1987": a retro TV weather map from a VHS tape. LEFT: country outline, isobar lines, sun and cloud symbols. MIDDLE: test card / end-of-broadcast screen. RIGHT: a giant hurricane spiral made of halftone dots rotating over the map, lightning symbols, distorted broadcast.`
8. **Liftoff.** `Concept 8 – "Countdown and liftoff": rocket on a launch pad in a synthwave desert at sunset. LEFT: countdown numbers, steam rising (text allowed: numbers only). MIDDLE: weightlessness in space, objects floating slowly, plates wobbling out of register. RIGHT: liftoff, massive fluo-orange exhaust plume, ground shaking, shockwave rings.`
9. **Walkman.** `Concept 9 – "Walkman cassette": extreme close-up of a cassette in a walkman. LEFT: reels turning, tape running, clean. MIDDLE: tape spaghetti — the tape pouring out and forming abstract dancing loops. RIGHT: full energy, reels spinning wildly, tape loops exploding outward like ribbons, heavy VHS distortion.`
10. **Garden.** `Concept 10 – "Time-lapse garden": a single plant growing from dark soil. LEFT: sprout with a few leaves, stem rising. MIDDLE: night, fireflies in the dark, closed bud. RIGHT: a huge flower bursting open into halftone dots in all four fluo colors, petals flying.`
